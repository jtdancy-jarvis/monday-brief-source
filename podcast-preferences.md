## HOW TO SPEND THE TWO QUESTIONS

Airtime is the scarce channel. Two questions a week, and each one costs about
forty words of an episode that also has to carry culture, sports and Disney.

**Spend them only on asks no button can collect.**

The Weekly Queue renders a Landed/Missed/Queued/Finished button for every pick
in `week_picks`. Anything of the form *did this specific thing land* belongs
there, not here — that channel handles ten a week and costs nothing.

What is left for airtime is the stuff a button cannot express:

- **Preferences and thresholds.** How much UNC detail. Whether the runtime is right.
- **Behaviour.** Whether he actually took the subscription month. Whether he rereads.
- **Meta.** Which segment he skips. Which he would grow.
- **Old asks with no current pick.** Anything predating `week_picks` has no
  button and can only be asked aloud. The Brothers Hawthorne is the standing
  example.

If you find yourself drafting an on-air question about a pick from this week's
episode, stop — put a button on it instead.

Rotate categories. Never repeat a question within eight weeks. The register
below makes that mechanical; do not try to enforce it by re-reading the log.
