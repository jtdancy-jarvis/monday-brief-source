# assets

`intro.wav`, `outro.wav`, `transition.wav` are SYNTHESIZED PLACEHOLDERS,
generated by `audio_post.py make-assets`. They are functional, not good.

Replace them with real music. Anything here is used as-is, so match the
filenames and keep the intro under about six seconds.

Genuinely free sources, check the licence on each track:

- Uppbeat (uppbeat.io) free tier, no attribution on most tracks
- Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music) Pixabay licence
- YouTube Audio Library, filter to no-attribution
- Free Music Archive (freemusicarchive.org), filter by CC licence
- Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC-BY, attribution required

Do not use commercial music. The feed is public and hosted on GitHub.
