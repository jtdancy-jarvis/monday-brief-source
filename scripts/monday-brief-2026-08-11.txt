Golf. The playoffs start this week.

The St. Jude Championship is Thursday through Sunday at TPC Southwind in Memphis. Lead with the ground: Southwind is flat, tight and water heavy, a course that punishes a loose driver rather than a short one, which is an unfashionable thing for a playoff venue to ask.

Then it moves to Bellerive, outside St. Louis, the week after. Bellerive is a Robert Trent Jones Senior course, opened in nineteen sixty and later sharpened by Rees Jones. Big, long, physical, and completely honest about what it is. Jones built courses that ask you to hit it a long way and then hit it straight, with no clever way around either question, and the modern taste for width and angles is in large part a reaction against exactly that. It has not hosted this event since two thousand eight.
