Sports. Football leads.

North Carolina beat twenty fifth ranked Pittsburgh twenty one to seventeen on Saturday. It was the first time Belichick has beaten a ranked opponent at Carolina, and the first ranked win for the program since twenty twenty three. It was also the first ranked road win since twenty twenty.

Pitt had just taken a seventeen to fourteen lead in the second half. Carolina answered at once. Quarterback Billy Edwards Junior found Shamar Easter down the seam for seventy five yards and the go ahead score. The defense then held the lead the rest of the way.

Edwards finished eighteen of twenty six for two hundred and forty nine yards and two touchdowns, with Jelani Thurman catching the other. He also threw two interceptions, which is the part to clean up. Pitt was without its starting quarterback. Holden Geriner made his first college start in his place.

The record is three and two overall, and one and one in the ACC. That is the first league win of the year, after the Notre Dame loss last week and the Clemson loss in September.

Next comes the one that matters in this part of the state. Carolina plays at Duke on Saturday, October seventeenth, in Durham, for the Victory Bell. I could not confirm a kickoff time or network, so check the listing. It is a rivalry game on the road, and the stretch is not over. Notre Dame and Pitt are behind Carolina now.

Then Syracuse at Kenan on the twenty fourth, and Miami on Halloween. After that the schedule eases a little. UConn on the road November seventh, Louisville at home on the fourteenth, Virginia on the road on the twenty first, and NC State at Kenan on Thanksgiving weekend, November twenty eighth.

Think about the arc for a moment. Carolina started two and one, lost to the third ranked team in the country, and has now beaten a ranked team on the road. At three and two with seven games left, a bowl game is a real target again. The offense under Bobby Petrino is built on the veer and shoot, and Saturday showed both halves of it, the quick strike and the turnover risk. Cut the interceptions and this team is better than its record suggests.

Basketball, headlines only. Michael Malone's first season continues with exhibitions. Carolina plays at Indiana on October eighteenth, and hosts Charlotte on October twenty seventh. The real opener is November second against Western Carolina. Those dates are from the program's schedule, and I would check them again before you plan around them.

The Panthers are off. They beat Detroit thirty two to twenty six on Sunday night last week, behind two Bryce Young touchdown passes to Tetairoa McMillan and a fifteen yard Chuba Hubbard run. They are two and two. They have a bye this Sunday, which matters, because the injury list is long. Cornerbacks Jaycee Horn and Mike Jackson are on injured reserve for about eight weeks. Rookie tackle Monroe Freeling hurt a knee, and linebacker Jaelan Phillips left with a hamstring. Next Sunday, October eighteenth, they play at the Eagles at one o'clock, according to the team's schedule. Philadelphia will be the first road test since the win over Detroit, and the secondary is the question. A bye helps, but it does not heal injured reserve.

Disney. A calendar story again, because October rewards planning.

Mickey's Not So Scary Halloween Party is still running selected nights at Magic Kingdom, and Halloween Time wraps up at the end of the month. The EPCOT Food and Wine Festival runs through November twenty first. Those are listed schedules, not rumors.

The new item is on the ships. Disney Cruise Line has confirmed that the stage show Disney Seas the Adventure will return aboard the Disney Believe. It is the fourth ship in the Wish class, set to launch in twenty twenty seven. The show has already run on the Wish, Treasure, Destiny and Adventure. Disney has not said whether any scenes or songs will change, so treat this as a placeholder on the schedule, not a preview.

Two smaller items from the blogs. Blog Mickey reports that Imagineering has filed permits for new signage at all four Walt Disney World parking plazas. That is a permit filing, not a Disney announcement, so there is no opening date and no detail on what the signs will say. And a Hallmark pop-up shop opened October seventh at Disney Springs, if holiday cards are on your list.

One more park note. Passholders had their first Good to Go day of the month last Tuesday. Disney adds those dates in batches, so the calendar is worth a look if a weekday visit is on your mind.
